Voxo Privacy Policy

Effective November 24, 2026

Voxo is a podcast, radio and audiobook player for Android. In short: Voxo has no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and the developer does not receive any information about you or what you listen to.

Information stored on your phone

Your subscriptions, listening progress, playlists, bookmarks and their notes, saved radio stations, audiobook library, settings and downloaded episodes are stored only on your phone, inside Voxo's own storage. They are not sent to the developer. Uninstalling Voxo deletes them. If Android's device backup is turned on, it may include this data; that backup is handled by Google under your Google account settings.

Your own audio files

If you import files or folders, Voxo reads them where they are, using the access you grant in Android's file picker; nothing is copied or uploaded. If you use "Find on this phone", Voxo asks for Android's permission to read audio files, and uses it only to list and play the audio on your phone. Titles and cover art read from your files are stored only on your phone.

Services Voxo connects to

To do its job, Voxo connects directly from your phone to these services. Like any website, they can see your device's IP address and the request being made. Voxo does not send them your name, email, contacts, location or any account information.

  • Podcast publishers and hosting services: to check the shows you follow for new episodes, and to stream or download episodes, chapter lists and transcripts.
  • Apple's public podcast directory (itunes.apple.com): to search for podcasts, show category charts, and find a show's Apple Podcasts link when you share it. The words you search for, or the show's title when you share it, are sent to get results.
  • Radio Browser (radio-browser.info): a free community directory of radio stations, to list and search stations. When you play a station, Voxo reports the play to the directory's popularity count, without anything that identifies you.
  • Radio stations: to stream the station you choose.
  • LibriVox and the Internet Archive (librivox.org, archive.org): to search free public-domain audiobooks, and to stream or download the ones you add.
  • Google Cast (through Google Play services): only when you choose to play on a speaker or TV.
  • Android Auto: only when you connect your phone to a car.

Each of these services has its own privacy policy.

Sharing

When you share a show, episode, station or audiobook, Voxo creates the message and, if you choose, an image card on your phone, then hands it to the app you pick in Android's share menu. Voxo sends nothing anywhere itself.

Permissions

  • Notifications: playback controls and, if you turn it on, alerts about new episodes.
  • Internet and network state: to load podcasts, radio and audiobooks.
  • Background playback (foreground service): so audio keeps playing with the screen off.
  • Read audio files (only when you use "Find on this phone"): to list the audio on your phone.
  • Vibration: a short buzz when you pick up a show to rearrange your list.

Voxo does not use the advertising ID.

Children

Voxo is not directed at children under 13. Podcasts, radio stations and audiobooks are made by third parties and may contain content intended for adults.
